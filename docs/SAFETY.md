# Safety and community standards

Respawn is intended for a broad gaming audience. Safety is a core feature, not a later decoration.

## Required controls before public launch
- Block and mute users; blocked users cannot DM, invite or mention the blocker.
- Report users, messages, profiles, posts, guilds and communities.
- Moderator queue with reason, evidence, status and audit history.
- Community owner/moderator roles with least-privilege permissions.
- Invite controls, message requests and configurable discoverability.
- Rate limits for sign-up, DMs, invites, posts, reactions and uploads.
- Link filtering and protection against phishing, impersonation and spam.
- Account export and deletion workflows.
- Clear privacy settings for profile, game activity and online presence.
- Age-appropriate defaults and a documented process for handling reports involving minors.
- Terms, privacy notice, retention policy and contact route before public release.

## Minimum community rules
1. No harassment, hate, threats, doxxing or targeted abuse.
2. No scams, phishing, malware, account trading or credential requests.
3. No sexual exploitation or inappropriate contact with minors.
4. Respect intellectual property and platform rules when sharing clips or images.
5. Keep recruitment, LFG and promotional posts in their intended spaces.
6. Moderators must explain enforcement actions and provide an appeal route where practical.

## Data minimisation
Collect only what the feature needs. Do not store platform passwords. Avoid collecting precise location. Make online status and linked account details opt-in and controllable.
